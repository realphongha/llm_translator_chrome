How to enable Chrome Built-in AI (One-time setup):

  1. Step 1: Turn on "On-device AI" in Chrome Settings:
    chrome://settings/ai

    Ensure the master toggle for On-device AI (or AI Innovations) is switched On.

  2. Step 2: In Chrome Flags, enable the Prompt API:
    chrome://flags/#prompt-api

    Set to Enabled, then click Relaunch at the bottom of Chrome.

  3. Step 3: Download Gemini Nano:

    Click the Download Gemini Nano button above, or check chrome://components (Optimization Guide On Device Model).

  4. Step 4 (Optional): Boost inference speed:
    chrome://flags/#on-device-model-speculative-decoding

    Enabling speculative decoding gives a 1.5x–2x speedup on local models.
